# Business Rules

*   **Grid Days Completion:** Registering two or more deposits on the same day does **not** increase the number of days completed on the Grid. It still counts as a single day of progress.
*   **Streak Protection (Lifebuoys):** If a user forgets to make a deposit, a "Lifebuoy" can be automatically or manually consumed to protect and maintain their active Streak.
*   **Cooperative Challenge Merge:** In a cooperative session, the progress of both users is merged or summed up to reach the shared goal. Both users contribute to the same progress pool.
*   **Economy Transactions:** All deductions or additions to a user's economy (Wooden Coins, Lifebuoys) must be handled explicitly through centralized methods in the `SavingsProvider` to ensure consistency.
